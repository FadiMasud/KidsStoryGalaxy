Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 4, 2026

Summary: Kids Story Galaxy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. All app data stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no tracking.

About This App

Kids Story Galaxy is an interactive storybook application designed for children ages 2–8, featuring narrated stories, mini-games, coloring activities, and achievement badges. The App is developed by Fadi Masud and is available on the Google Play Store.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information from any user, including children. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • No user accounts, registration, or login of any kind
  • No photographs, videos, or audio recordings
  • No location or geolocation data
  • No device identifiers, IP addresses, or persistent identifiers
  • No cookies, analytics, diagnostics, or usage data transmitted off-device

Local Data Storage

To provide its features, the App stores data locally on your device only using Android SharedPreferences and app-internal storage:

  • Reading progress, earned stars, and game scores
  • Coloring artwork saved as images
  • Achievement badges and reading streak history
  • User preferences (language, theme, age group, narration settings)
  • Subscription status (verified via Google Play Billing)

This data is never transmitted, uploaded, or shared with any server, service, or third party. All local data can be removed by uninstalling the App.

Data Retention & Deletion

All data is stored exclusively on your device. The App has no servers, databases, or cloud storage. Data is retained only for as long as the App is installed. To delete all data, simply uninstall the App from your device. There is no additional data to request deletion of, as nothing is stored externally.

Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any data, there is no risk of data breach from our services. All data stored locally on your device is protected by your device’s built-in security features, including screen lock, encryption, and any biometric authentication you have configured.

Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Families Policy)

Kids Story Galaxy fully complies with COPPA, GDPR for children, and Google’s Families Policy:

  • No personal information is collected from children or any user
  • No user accounts or login required
  • No social features, chat, messaging, or user-generated content sharing
  • No behavioral tracking or profiling
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No data is transmitted to any external server

Advertising

Kids Story Galaxy contains no advertisements of any kind. There are no ad SDKs, no ad networks, and no sponsored content. The App is entirely ad-free.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party services for analytics, advertising, tracking, or data collection. The only external service interaction is:

  • Google Play Billing: Used solely for processing subscription purchases. This is handled by the Android operating system. We do not have access to your payment information or Google account details.

No data about your child or your usage of the App is shared with any third party.

Parental Rights

As a parent or guardian, you can remove all locally stored data by uninstalling the App. Purchases require Google account authentication and parental approval when configured through Google Family Link. Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing to review, delete, or restrict.

International Users (GDPR)

  • Data Controller: Fadi Masud
  • We do not process any personal data. No legal basis is required.
  • No personal data is transferred internationally or to any server.
  • You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority at any time.

Google Play Data Safety

In accordance with Google Play’s Data Safety requirements, our store listing declares:

  • No data is collected
  • No data is shared with third parties
  • Data cannot be deleted because no data is collected
  • No data is transferred to third parties

This is consistent with our privacy practices described above.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. If we ever begin collecting data, we will provide prominent notice and obtain required consents, including verifiable parental consent as required by COPPA.

Support & FAQ

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Sign in with the same Google account used for the original purchase. The App will automatically detect your active subscription.

How do I change the language?

Open the Parent Area (solve the math challenge) and scroll to the Language section. Choose from 6 supported languages: English, Arabic, Spanish, Czech, German, and French.

Can my child use the app offline?

Yes! All stories, narration, games, and coloring pages are included in the app. No internet connection is needed after download.

Is the app safe for children?

Absolutely. The App collects no data, has no ads, no social features, and no in-app communication. Premium features are protected by a Parent Gate (math challenge). We comply with COPPA and Google’s Families Policy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Google Play Store > tap your profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > find Kids Story Galaxy > Cancel subscription.

What age group is the app for?

Kids Story Galaxy is designed for children ages 2–8, with content organized into three age groups: Toddler (2–3), Preschool (4–5), and Early Reader (6–8).

How do I contact support?
